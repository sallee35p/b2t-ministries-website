Our story

Between the trees.

A name that invites us to slow down, notice God’s creation, and remember where we stand in the great story of Scripture.

Why the name matters

We live between the trees.

There is something about walking among trees that invites us to slow down—to step away from noise, notice what God has made, and make room to listen. That sense of coming away is part of the heart behind Between the Trees.

But the name also points beyond the landscape to the whole story of Scripture. In Genesis, humanity is given access to the Tree of Life in the presence of God (Genesis 2:9). Sin brings separation, exile, and loss of access to the tree (Genesis 3:22–24). Yet God does not abandon His creation.

At the center of the biblical story stands another tree—the cross—where Jesus gives His life to reconcile sinners to God.

And in Revelation, the Tree of Life appears again (Revelation 22:1–2, 14) as God makes all things new and dwells with His people forever.

We live between the trees—redeemed by Christ, being formed in His likeness, and sent into the world as His people while we await the restoration of all things.

Scripture-centered

Christ-focused

Formation is ultimately about becoming more like Jesus and living faithfully in Him.

Church-connected

Between the Trees exists to serve alongside the local church, not replace it.

Purposefully sent

What God forms in us is meant to shape our families, churches, vocations, and communities.

Our mission

Encounter God. Be formed. Live sent.

Between the Trees exists to help people encounter God through His Word, be formed in the way of Christ, and live faithfully for His purposes.

We want every resource, gathering, and future place we steward to move people toward deeper engagement with Scripture, healthier Christian community, and faithful everyday discipleship.
