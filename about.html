Our story

Between two trees.

A ministry shaped by the whole biblical story—from creation and exile to redemption in Christ and the restoration of all things.

Why the name matters

The story of Scripture unfolds between two trees.

In Genesis, humanity is given access to the Tree of Life in the presence of God (Genesis 2:9). Sin brings separation, exile, and loss of access to the tree (Genesis 3:22–24). Yet God does not abandon His creation.

At the center of the biblical story stands another tree—the cross—where Jesus gives His life to reconcile sinners to God.

And in Revelation, the Tree of Life appears again (Revelation 22:1–2, 14) as God makes all things new and dwells with His people forever.

We live between those trees—redeemed by Christ, being formed in His likeness, and sent into the world as His people while we await the restoration of all things.

Scripture-centered

God’s Word remains the authority and the foundation for what we teach and build.

Christ-focused

Formation is ultimately about becoming more like Jesus and living faithfully in Him.

Church-connected

B2T exists to serve alongside the local church, not replace it.

Purposefully sent

What God forms in us is meant to shape our families, churches, vocations, and communities.

Our mission

Encounter God. Be formed. Live sent.

Between Two Trees Ministries exists to help people encounter God through His Word, be formed in the way of Christ, and live faithfully for His purposes.

We want every resource, gathering, and future place we steward to move people toward deeper engagement with Scripture, healthier Christian community, and faithful everyday discipleship.
