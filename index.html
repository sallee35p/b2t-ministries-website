The story behind the name

We live between two trees.

From the Tree of Life in Genesis (Genesis 2:9; 3:22–24) to the Tree of Life in Revelation (Revelation 22:1–2, 14), Scripture tells one great story of creation, fall, redemption, and restoration—with the cross of Jesus Christ standing at its center.

Our ministry rhythm

Rooted in Scripture.

Everything B2T builds is intended to help people move from encountering God in His Word to becoming more like Christ and carrying that formation into everyday life.

Featured ministry

Scripture was meant to be explored.

Scripture Guide helps you engage God’s Word when you want to understand a passage, seek biblical guidance for something you’re facing, or explore what Scripture teaches about an important question.

Scripture Guide isn’t designed to replace Scripture, your pastor, or the local church. It’s designed to help you engage Scripture more deeply and confidently.

The vision

Creating places to come away.

We believe there is value in intentionally stepping away from the noise of everyday life to seek God through Scripture, prayer, worship, community, and creation.

B2T is working toward a place where youth, families, churches, and ministry leaders can come away, draw near to God, be formed in Christ, and return home prepared to live sent.
