Privacy

How Between Two Trees Ministries intends to handle information shared through this website.

A simple privacy posture

This public website is currently informational and does not intentionally collect personal information through forms, accounts, or online giving.

As B2T adds contact forms, donation tools, analytics, or other services, this page will be updated to explain what information is collected, why it is used, how it is protected, and what choices are available to visitors.

Third-party services

Future features may rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, analytics, donations, forms, or the Scripture Guide. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy practices.

Contact

Before public launch, this policy will be finalized alongside the ministry’s official contact information and production services.

Pre-launch note: This page is a working policy summary and should be reviewed once the final contact, donation, analytics, and Scripture Guide services are connected.
