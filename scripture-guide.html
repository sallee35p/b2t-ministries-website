Three ways to explore

Built around the question you’re actually asking.

Instead of beginning with a generic chatbot, Scripture Guide starts by helping you choose the kind of biblical exploration you need.

Passage

Understand what you’re reading.

Explore context, central truth, Scripture connections, and deeper study without losing sight of the passage itself.

Guidance

Bring life questions to Scripture.

Find biblical passages and pathways relevant to what you are facing, with answers that lead you back to God’s Word.

Theology

Explore what Scripture teaches.

Study important doctrines while distinguishing explicit biblical teaching, reasonable inference, and debated interpretations.

The design philosophy

A guide to Scripture—not a replacement for it.

Scripture Guide is intended to make biblical exploration more accessible while preserving the right order of authority: Scripture first, healthy Christian community alongside it, and technology in service to both.

1

Scripture is the authority. The tool exists to help users read and understand the biblical text more deeply.

2

AI serves the exploration. It can route questions, synthesize material, personalize study, and support deeper inquiry.

3

The local church still matters. Scripture Guide should strengthen—not substitute for—pastors, teachers, and Christian community.

Product posture

Charity without doctrinal vagueness.

The goal is to be clear where Scripture is clear, appropriately cautious where faithful Christians disagree, and consistently transparent about the difference between biblical text and interpretive conclusion.
