Terms

Basic terms for using the Between Two Trees Ministries website and resources.

This website exists to share information about Between Two Trees Ministries, its developing resources, and its future vision. Content is provided for ministry, educational, and informational purposes.

B2T resources are intended to encourage deeper engagement with Scripture and healthy participation in the local church. They are not a substitute for pastoral care, professional counseling, medical care, legal advice, or other qualified professional services where those are needed.

Features such as online giving, contact forms, downloadable resources, and Scripture Guide may introduce additional terms or third-party service conditions. Those terms will be added or linked when the services become publicly available.

Content use

Unless otherwise stated, original B2T website content and resources remain the property of Between Two Trees Ministries. Public ministry resources may include specific permissions for personal, church, or group use when they are released.
