The heart of the vision

Creating places to come away.

B2T is working toward a future ministry setting where youth, families, churches, and ministry leaders can step away from the noise of everyday life to seek God through Scripture, prayer, worship, community, and creation.

The goal is not escape for its own sake. The goal is intentional space for encounter and formation that sends people back into ordinary life with renewed clarity and purpose.

A vision in development

From outdoor gathering spaces to The Summit.

The long-term vision includes camps, retreats, outdoor gathering spaces, and eventually The Summit—an intentional ministry environment shaped around renewal in God’s presence and faithful return to His purposes.

These ideas will be developed in phases as the ministry, property, partnerships, and resources mature.

Important: These physical ministries and facilities are part of the vision being developed. This site does not present them as currently operating programs.
Why place matters

Formation often needs intentional space.

Throughout Scripture, God often uses particular places and intentional moments to call His people away from distraction, meet with them, form them, and send them.

B2T hopes to steward physical space in a way that serves those purposes while remaining firmly connected to the ministry of the local church.

The Summit

Renewed by His Presence. Sent for His Purpose.

The Summit is envisioned as one future expression of the broader B2T mission—not the whole of the ministry, but a place where the B2T rhythm can be embodied in a particular setting.
